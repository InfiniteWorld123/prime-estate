# Prime Estate

## Product Vision

Prime Estate is a real estate agency platform that helps a single agency
organize, publish, and market properties, then manage potential customers
until a property is sold or rented.

## Users

### Visitor

- Browses published property listings.
- Views property details.
- Sends an inquiry.
- Books a property viewing appointment.

### Admin

- Manages properties and listings.
- Publishes and archives listings.
- Reviews and manages inquiries.
- Manages viewing appointments.
- Tracks leads until they are won or lost.

## Core Workflow

1. The admin adds a property and prepares its listing.
2. The admin publishes the listing.
3. A visitor discovers and views the listing.
4. The visitor sends an inquiry or books a viewing.
5. The admin follows up with the lead.
6. The property is sold or rented.
7. The listing is closed and archived.

## MVP Scope

- Admin authentication
- Property and listing management
- Public property listings
- Property inquiries
- Viewing appointment booking
- Basic lead management
- Listing closing and archiving

## Not in the MVP

- Multiple organizations
- Staff roles and permissions
- Owner and customer accounts
- Contracts and payments
- Advanced ownership management
- Blogging
- Analytics
- Third-party integrations

## MVP Success

The MVP is complete when an admin can publish a property listing,
a visitor can send an inquiry or book a viewing, and the admin can
follow that lead until the listing is sold, rented, or archived.
